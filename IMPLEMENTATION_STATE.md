Goal: our custom components behave like iOS system ones, still looking like the desktop app but moving like iOS. **General rule for all mobile motion: copy Apple's iOS animation style.** Custom animations that feel interactive and physical, driven by springs and by the finger rather than by canned timelines. The most important property of great iOS animation is that it is **interruptible and cancellable**: touch a moving sheet, menu or drawer and it stops under the finger at its current position and velocity (nothing jumps or restarts); let go and it **springs** to wherever the gesture points, carrying the release velocity; dragging is always 1 to 1 with the finger with rubber banding at the limits; a tap during an animation reverses it smoothly instead of waiting for it to finish.
