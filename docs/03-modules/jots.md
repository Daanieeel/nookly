# Module: Jots

- **Jot:** a raw capture made in the moment, during a lecture for example.

A Jot is refined into a regular Note. The two are linked through the standard generic relationship system, in either direction. A Jot with no linked Note is unrefined. There is no dedicated pairing structure and no separate "polished" page type.

"Refine into New Note" (context menu, the page's overflow menu, and the top of the right sidebar) creates a Note titled like the Jot, links them with `relates-to`, and opens the Note.

## Layout Direction

**Jot:** a fast, minimal capture surface with almost no chrome and a large text area. Built for speed.

## Creation UX

Creating a Jot must feel instant. One keystroke or click from anywhere opens a blank capture surface with no friction.

## The Jots Page

An inbox: it opens on the Jots still to refine, and Show refined adds the refined ones under them in a group of their own. One toolbar row holds the search, Show refined and Filter; the chip row appears only once a filter beyond those is added. Every unrefined row has a Refine button on hover, and `R` on a focused row does the same. Columns nothing fills (Preview, Session) are left out. The phone has the same: Unrefined by default and a Show refined chip.
